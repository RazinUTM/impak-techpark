## Before the session
- Open the published page on the projector laptop; press ⓘ once to check the screen guide.
- Facilitator → *Open the console* with your e-mail (listed in the `users` tab) and the GM PIN. Set level, teams, pace, deal window; *Create game*. The bar shows the **room code** and each **team PIN** — write them on cards.
- *Front screen* on the projector (shows the room code until the clock starts).
- Teams open the page on any browser → *Join a live session* → room code, team PIN, name, e-mail. Phones work; one laptop per team is better.

## During
1. Start the clock when every team has joined. Pace 45–60 s per month for a first group.
2. Deal Room cards hold the clock; they close by themselves after the window (or *Close now*).
3. Announce checkpoints and the forecast; use *Message to every screen* for reminders.
4. At the final bell (Bina, Lestari): hear each team's two-minute defence, mark D in the console, then *Reveal medals*.

## After
- *Download results (CSV)* and *Download order log (JSON)* from the console; the `results` tab also holds the server replay (official score, flag `ok` / `mismatch`).
- Debrief questions: which part scored lowest and why; one order you would reverse; what the park's drift says about the mandate; where ESG was bought late.
- Delete participants' rows from `players`, `codes` and `log` 12 months after the cohort report.
